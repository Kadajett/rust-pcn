Graphs and progress

The dashboard reports what the running trainer has actually written. It distinguishes model energy, corpus progress, throughput, checkpoint state, and sampled predictions.

Positive energy

Prediction-error energy after settling the clean input with selected output targets clamped. It is the supervised positive phase.

Free energy

Prediction-error energy after settling corrupted input with outputs free. It is not a language loss or a human quality score.

Interpretation. Stable finite energy shows numerical health. It does not prove that generated text is correct. Output samples and task evaluations are separate evidence.

Current cumulative stage contract

Atomic expert continuation

Each durable save writes both 139,899,456-parameter experts into a new generation, syncs their independent SEAL state, and only then atomically advances experts.json. A partial generation is never resumed.

One resident expert

The inherited expert is copied back to CPU before the request-conditioned expert enters GPU memory. The measured production allocation remains below the six-gigabyte device limit.

Fixed holdouts

One task record in twenty is reserved deterministically and never enters task losses. Evenly distributed fixed subsets drive typed, sequence, and vision-language promotion gates after each stage.

Forward lineage

Both experts began as bit-identical copies of the newest durable v5 checkpoint. Subsequent routed updates continue forward; they do not modify or replace that immutable source.

One cumulative training stage

Load the inherited expert and read bounded contiguous cursor slices from active and rehearsal corpora.
Train masked-contrastive text, code, image, and legacy-control batches under inherited update masks.
Copy the inherited expert to CPU, release its GPU tensors, and load the request-conditioned expert.
Train typed soft targets, response tokens, vision-language targets, structured output, and generic Noul requests.
Evaluate fixed held-out typed Brier/ranking and sequence/vision token accuracy on the request-conditioned expert.
Commit both experts and their independent SEAL states as one atomic checkpoint generation.

Public live results and probes

The universal trainer emits supervised request-conditioned Noul predictions at the configured sample interval and emits named runtime probes submitted through the dashboard. Inherited and task-aware batches contribute aggregate energy, progress, and held-out promotion telemetry.

No API key required. Live samples load automatically, and anyone can submit a bounded model probe. Samples and probes may contain local corpus text and are publicly visible; do not submit secrets. One model probe runs at a time, between safe training batches. Only Friday-update publication remains authenticated so visitors cannot overwrite the latest work update.

Jev quality audit

A separate low-frequency service batches recent output samples and sends one typed TypeSafe Jev request. Local grading preserves each observable contract: Noul uses MAE and Brier and passes only at MAE ≤ 0.10; endpoint predictions within 0.0001 automatically fail for soft targets between 0.05 and 0.95. Choice records selected-label correctness, unique ranking, ties, margin, and target probability; Score separates point MAE from distribution rank; structured output reports exact-object and field accuracy; prose and code use exact next-byte accuracy.

Strict contract graph

The aggregate percentage counts only complete contract passes. Saturated Noul answers to soft targets are explicit failures. A correct Choice label or Score point with a tied distribution is partial evidence and is not promoted to a full pass.

Correlation

After at least three audits, the dashboard reports Pearson correlation between free energy and both strict contract pass rate and Jev quality. It is observational and does not establish causation.

Training gate

Audit prompts and Jev answers remain private evaluation records. They enter training only through an explicit accepted synthetic wave after real datasets are exhausted.

Cadence

The UI refreshes every ten seconds. Jev requests are batched separately so browser refreshes do not spend credits or alter training.

Checkpoint and failure rules

  • The energy ceiling remains 10,000,000; the active dual-expert run settles every batch for 40 steps, then only batches still above the ceiling continue adaptively up to 160 steps before stopping.
  • A violating batch stops before a non-finite update can be checkpointed.
  • Every committed generation contains both complete experts, synchronized counters and cursors, and independent SEAL state.
  • The manifest changes only after both checkpoint directories are durable; obsolete generations are removed afterward.
  • Task-path updates preserve protected inherited parameters; dedicated CPU and GPU tests compare those tensors exactly.
  • Only persisted fixed-gate results may enable new runtime output paths.
